# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers in the New Zealand tech market. They have real experience and skills but struggle to tailor their CV to each role. They paste a job offer and want a CV that is honest, precise, and ready to submit — not inflated or invented.

## Product Purpose

TrueFit generates tailored CVs and cover letters from a skill inventory the user builds once. The AI selects and frames only what the user has actually listed — it never fabricates. Success means the user walks away with a document they'd be proud to submit.

## Brand Personality

Warm, Trustworthy, Simple.

Voice: calm and direct. No hype. No "supercharge your career" copy. Speaks like a competent colleague who helps you put your best foot forward.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS: cream/sand backgrounds, gradient CTA buttons, 01/02/03 section eyebrows, hero-metric grids, "AI-powered" badges everywhere.
- Corporate HR software: navy dashboards, enterprise visual density, cold and bureaucratic.
- Flashy startup landing pages: neon gradients, large hero numbers, overdesigned.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through transparency** — every design choice should reinforce that the AI does what it says. No decorative complexity that makes the tool feel like a black box.
2. **Warmth without decoration** — approachable and human, but restraint wins over ornamentation. A warm tone is carried by copy, spacing, and color — not by illustration or emoji.
3. **One job per screen** — each page reveals only what the user needs at that moment. No anticipatory complexity.
4. **Earned simplicity** — whitespace and negative space are deliberate, not empty. Every element present earns its place.
5. **Confidence without hype** — the product is quietly capable. Let the output speak.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Supports keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Designed for screen legibility on laptop/desktop first (NZ office context); responsive down to mobile.
